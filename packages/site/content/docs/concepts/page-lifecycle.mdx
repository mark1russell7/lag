`LifecycleStateMachine` tracks the Page Lifecycle state of the page: active, passive, hidden, frozen or terminated. Browsers throttle timers and stop animation frames in hidden tabs. For this reason, the timer, frame and idle monitors pause while the page is hidden or frozen.
